## Generation prompts

### icon

Use case: precise-object-edit. Create a standalone square Google Play app icon from the LEFT icon in the supplied concept A board. Preserve that chosen design: bold gold compass rose, amber glowing diamond center, dark teal circular face, weathered dark outer ring, one small red gem at left point and blue gem at right point. Preserve its composition and premium cel-shaded fantasy finish. Refine for readability at small size: reduce scattered sparks and tiny texture, emphasize broad gold shapes and crisp silhouette. Output ONLY the icon artwork filling the entire square canvas edge to edge, no board, labels, title, margins, framing mockup or rounded corners. Opaque full-bleed deep teal background. Keep chosen design, do not replace it with a giant diamond or a character. Square high resolution.

### banner

Use case: precise-object-edit. Produce a standalone Google Play feature graphic by refining ONLY the right-hand banner from supplied concept C board. Preserve the chosen design and recognizable characters: brown-haired chibi knight in silver/gold armor and navy scarf at left, blonde chibi mage in teal/gold hood with purple crystal staff at right, readable gold title exactly "MythDusk" centered over subtle gold compass, large red blue green yellow purple resource gems flowing along lower foreground, dark teal fantasy dusk backdrop and restrained amber horizon. Maintain chibi proportions, outfits, clean cel-shaded style and palette. Wide composition at exactly 2.048:1 aspect ratio intended for 1024x500 final export; output 2048x1000 if possible. ONLY banner artwork full bleed, no presentation board, labels, margins or thin external border. All title letters, faces, staff crystal and essential gems inset comfortably at least 10% from edges, allow outer background to crop. Reduce small sparks and busy texture. No additional text, badges, slogans or UI. This is promotional illustration, not gameplay screenshot.
